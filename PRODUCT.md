# PRODUCT.md — The Barns Resort

## What this is
The brand website for **The Barns Resort**, a boutique 12-room luxury mountain resort in **Bhimtal, Uttarakhand** (Kumaon hills, near Nainital, India). Modern barn-style cottages with glass façades and A-frame timber ceilings, terraced on a hillside above Bhimtal Lake. Every room has a private balcony with a Himalayan/valley view.

## Audience
Affluent Indian domestic travellers (Delhi-NCR primarily) and couples/families planning a 2–4 night Kumaon escape: honeymooners, weekenders, remote-working couples, multi-generational families. They are mobile-first, comparison-shop on OTAs, and respond to atmosphere and trust signals (real photos, real reviews) more than feature lists.

## The job the site does
1. Make the property feel genuinely premium and distinctive in the first three seconds — the architecture and dusk lighting are the hero.
2. Communicate the "Raw & Luxury" character: rustic Kumaoni stone-and-timber craft fused with five-star comfort.
3. Show the three suite types honestly with real photography and real amenities.
4. Drive bookings to the resort's existing OTA listings (Booking.com, MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Agoda, EaseMyTrip, Airbnb) via a single elegant chooser, plus direct call/WhatsApp.
5. Rank for local intent: "resorts in bhimtal", "luxury resort bhimtal", "bhimtal lake hotel".

## Voice
Quiet, confident, editorial — like a travel magazine spread, not a hotel brochure. Short declarative sentences. Sensory, specific, grounded in the actual place (misty mornings, string-lit terraces, pine, slate, the lake below). Never hype, never emoji, never "elevate your stay ✨", never exclamation-stacked marketing.

## Hard truths to respect (do not invent)
- 12 rooms, 3 suite types: **Suite with Balcony**, **Mountain View Suite**, **Family Room with Attic**.
- **No pool, no spa, no gym.** Do not depict or promise them.
- Real amenities only: private balconies, AC, flat-screen TV, tea/coffee, en-suite bath, free Wi-Fi, free parking, in-house Indian restaurant, rooftop evening tea, bonfire/firepit, garden, children's play area & menu, board games.
- Check-in 12:00, check-out 11:00. No pets. Children welcome.
- One phone: **+91 87006 19502** (also WhatsApp). Address: Dungsil, Bhimtal Road, Bhimtal, Uttarakhand 263136.

## Anti-references (what it must NOT look like)
Generic SaaS landing pages, AI-slop template hotels, purple/indigo gradients, glassmorphism cards, three-equal-icon feature grids, Inter/Poppins everywhere, stock imagery, rounded-2xl drop-shadow card soup. If it could be any hotel, it's wrong.
